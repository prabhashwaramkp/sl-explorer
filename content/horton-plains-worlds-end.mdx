Horton Plains is a protected highland plateau of grassland and cloud forest, and its main draw — World's End — is a sheer 880-metre escarpment with views that, on a clear morning, stretch to the southern coast. By late morning, cloud typically rolls in and closes the view entirely.

## Why timing matters here more than almost anywhere

Arrive at the park gate by 6am. This isn't exaggeration for effect — by 9-10am, cloud cover reliably obscures the entire view, and many visitors who arrive later leave without seeing anything at all.

## The full loop

The circular trail also passes Baker's Falls and open plains where sambar deer are commonly spotted grazing in the early morning quiet.

## Pairing with the Pekoe Trail

This sits close enough to the upper Pekoe Trail stages (14 and beyond) to combine into a longer highland itinerary if you're already based in the area.
