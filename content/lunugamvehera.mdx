Lunugamvehera's real significance is ecological — it forms the critical elephant corridor linking [Yala](/yala-national-park) and [Udawalawe](/udawalawe-national-park), allowing herds to move between the two larger parks rather than being isolated.

## What to expect

A quieter, less developed safari experience than its more famous neighbors, with elephants, deer, and buffalo the main sightings. Most visitors treat it as part of a broader southern wildlife circuit rather than a standalone destination.

## Best time to go

May through September generally offers the best conditions.
