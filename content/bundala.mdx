A UNESCO biosphere reserve of coastal lagoons and wetlands, Bundala is Sri Lanka's most reliable spot for migratory flamingos and a strong supporting cast of waterbirds, crocodiles, and the occasional elephant.

## What to expect

November through March is peak migratory season, when bird numbers and diversity are at their highest. Jeep safaris here move at a slower, more observational pace than a typical leopard-focused drive.

## A natural pairing

Close enough to Tissamaharama and Hambantota to combine with a [Yala](/yala-national-park) safari, or with [Ussangoda](/ussangoda)'s coastal scenery on the same day.
