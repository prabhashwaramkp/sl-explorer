Built entirely without steel by local craftsmen during a WWI materials shortage, this colonial-era railway bridge has become the single most photographed spot in the hill country — and it's an easy walk from anywhere in Ella.

## Getting the shot without the crowd

The 9:45am train from Ella is the most popular to photograph, which also means the most crowded viewpoint. Walk 10 minutes further along the paddy-field path for a quieter angle with the same bridge in frame.

## Pairing it with your hike

If you're doing [Pekoe Trail Stage 16](/pekoe-trail-stage-16-ella), this is a natural add-on either before you start (it's a short walk from the town center) or as a cooldown after you finish.
