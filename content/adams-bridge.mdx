Known locally as Rama's Bridge, this chain of limestone shoals stretching toward India carries deep significance in the Ramayana — and today protects a shallow marine ecosystem of sandbanks, seagrass, and migratory birds.

## What to expect

A boat trip is required to properly explore the sandbanks, arranged via Mannar or Talaimannar. Access can be regulated, so check current arrangements before planning around it.

## Best time to go

January through April, when calmer, drier conditions make boat access more comfortable.
