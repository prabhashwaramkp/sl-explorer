If Yala is famous for leopards, Udawalawe is famous for near-guaranteed elephant sightings — the open grassland habitat around its central reservoir supports one of the densest wild elephant populations in Sri Lanka, with herds visible on the vast majority of safari drives.

## What to expect

The terrain here is more open than Yala's scrub and forest, meaning better visibility and often longer, clearer sightings rather than brief glimpses. Water buffalo, crocodiles, and a strong bird population round out the wildlife, though leopard sightings are rarer than at Yala.

## A good pairing

The Elephant Transit Home, a rehabilitation center for orphaned elephant calves, sits right next to the park entrance and makes a worthwhile add-on — feeding times are open to visitors at set hours.

## Best time to go

Less seasonally dependent than Yala, since the reservoir provides a reliable water source year-round — a reasonably good choice even outside peak dry months.
