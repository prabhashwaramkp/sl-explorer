Kaudulla shares the same interconnected tank-and-forest ecosystem as Minneriya, and elephants move between the two depending on water conditions — meaning whichever park currently has the gathering can shift within a season.

## What to expect

A similarly open reservoir landscape to Minneriya, with deer and a strong bird population alongside elephant herds. Generally quieter in terms of visitor traffic.

## Best time to go

August through October, particularly toward the end of the dry season. Worth checking locally which of Kaudulla or [Minneriya](/minneriya) currently has the larger gathering before booking.
