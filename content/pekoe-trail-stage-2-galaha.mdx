If Stage 1 is the physical opener, Stage 2 is the trail's first real story — passing directly through Loolkandura Estate, where James Taylor planted Sri Lanka's first commercial tea bushes in 1867, effectively starting the industry that still defines the hill country today.

## What to expect

Gentler underfoot than Stage 1, mostly estate roads and footpaths through active tea gardens. This is a good stage for slowing down rather than rushing through — the history here rewards a bit of time.

## Don't miss

If the estate is open when you pass through, a short tea-tasting stop is well worth the detour — see the [Loolkandura Estate guide](/loolkandura-tea-estate) for details.
