Chundikulam's lagoons and coastal wetlands sit well off the standard tourist circuit, rewarding the effort with some of the best migratory bird numbers anywhere in Sri Lanka.

## What to expect

November through March is peak migratory season, when wetland bird diversity is at its highest. Infrastructure here is minimal, so this suits travelers already comfortable with a more independent, less-developed visit.

## Getting there

Access is via Jaffna or Kilinochchi, making it a natural add-on to a northern Sri Lanka trip.
