At roughly 45km from Colombo, Horagolla is the most accessible national park on the island — a modest lowland forest better suited to a relaxed morning walk than a dedicated wildlife trip.

## What to expect

Birds and butterflies rather than large mammals. A good option if you have a spare half-day near Colombo and want a taste of protected forest without committing to a long drive.

## Best time to go

December through April tends to be drier and more comfortable.
