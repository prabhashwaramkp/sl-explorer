Sri Lanka has practiced Ayurveda continuously for over 3,000 years, and Bentota's cluster of dedicated retreats is where much of the country's modern wellness tourism industry took root — meaning genuinely experienced practitioners, not a recent trend dressed up for visitors.

## What an actual program looks like

Most retreats start with a consultation to determine your dosha (constitution type), which shapes a personalized plan — typically a combination of daily oil treatments (abhyanga), steam therapy, and a specific diet, rather than a single spa-day treatment. Programs commonly run a minimum of 3 days, with serious detox or therapeutic programs extending to 2 weeks or more.

## What to expect physically

Detox programs can involve dietary restriction and treatments that some visitors find intense (certain purgation therapies, for instance) — a good retreat will explain exactly what's involved before you commit, and shorter "wellness" programs exist for those wanting the relaxation benefits without the more intensive therapeutic elements.

## Choosing a retreat

Look for retreats with a resident, qualified Ayurvedic physician (not just therapists) if you want a genuine therapeutic program rather than a spa treatment. Session length, the doctor's actual credentials, and whether meals are cooked to your program's requirements are the practical questions worth asking upfront.
