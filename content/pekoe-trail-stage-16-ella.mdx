Stage 16 is where most people's Pekoe Trail plans start, and for good reason. It's the most accessible stage — Ella itself is already a major stop on the hill-country circuit — and it delivers the trail's signature view: tea terraces folding into ridgelines, with Namunukula's peak holding the horizon.

## What to expect

You'll start from central Ella, climbing gradually through smallholder tea gardens before the trail steepens toward the ridge. The middle section is the most exposed — bring sun protection even on overcast days, since the cloud cover here burns off fast. The final push to Namunukula is a proper climb, but it's short, and the summit view is the payoff for the whole stage.

**Best time to start:** before 7am. The light is best, the heat hasn't set in, and you'll likely have the ridge to yourself for the first hour.

## Trail status

Stage 16 has had no reported closures or damage as of the most recent trail organisation updates. Always check current conditions before you go, especially during monsoon months (May–September on this side of the island).

## Hiring a local guide

While this stage is walkable independently thanks to good trail markers, a local guide (typically arranged for around $50 for the stage) adds real value here — particularly for pointing out which estates are open for tea tastings along the way.
