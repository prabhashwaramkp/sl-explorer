On a clear morning, the view from Lipton's Seat reportedly stretches all the way to the southern coast — a claim that's hard to verify but easy to believe once you're standing there before the mist rolls in.

## Getting there

Most visitors combine it with a walk or tuk-tuk ride through the working Dambatenne Tea Factory estate, which still processes tea using much of its original early-1900s machinery.

## Best time to go

Arrive by 7am. Cloud cover typically builds through the morning and can obscure the view entirely by mid-day.

## Pairing it with your hike

A natural half-day add-on if you're basing yourself in Haputale for [Pekoe Trail Stages 12-14](/pekoe-trail-stage-12-haputale).
