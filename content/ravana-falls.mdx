Named for the Ramayana's Ravana, who local legend holds hid Sita in a nearby cave, this 25-metre waterfall is one of the most accessible in the hill country — visible right from the Ella–Wellawaya road.

## Practical notes

Flow is strongest just after monsoon season (roughly October–December); by mid-year it can slow to a trickle. The pool at the base is safe for a shallow dip but currents strengthen quickly with recent rain — ask locally before swimming after heavy weather.

## Combine it with

This pairs naturally with a [Pekoe Trail Stage 16](/pekoe-trail-stage-16-ella) day — most people stop here on the drive back into Ella.
