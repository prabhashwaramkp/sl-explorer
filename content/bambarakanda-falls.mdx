The tallest waterfall in Sri Lanka, Bambarakanda drops in a single, narrow, dramatic column through pine and cloud forest. Unlike Diyaluma, there's no climb involved — the main viewpoint is a short, easy walk from the parking area.

## Best time to visit

Flow is strongest from November through February. By the dry mid-year months it can thin considerably, so if a full, powerful waterfall is the goal, plan around the wetter months.

## Practical notes

A small entry fee applies. It's a quieter, less crowded stop than Diyaluma, making it a good choice if you'd rather skip the queues.
