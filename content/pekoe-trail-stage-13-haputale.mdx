Stage 13 is one of the trail's less-walked sections, which is exactly its appeal if you've already done the busier Ella stages. The route moves from open tea estate into patches of cloud forest as it approaches Ohiya, with noticeably cooler, damper air the further you go.

## What to expect

Underfoot conditions change more here than on most stages — dry estate roads give way to muddier forest trail, especially after rain. Proper footwear matters more on this stage than most.

## Why it's worth doing

If you're chasing quiet trail time rather than landmark photo stops, this is one of the better stages for it. Wildlife sightings, including the occasional sambar deer, are more common here than on the more populated Ella stretch.
