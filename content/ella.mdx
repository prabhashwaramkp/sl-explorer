Ella is where most travelers get their first real taste of Sri Lanka's hill country — close enough to reach easily by road or the scenic hill-country railway, small enough to explore on foot, and surrounded by enough trekking, waterfalls, and viewpoints to fill several days without repeating yourself.

## What Ella is actually like

Despite its size, Ella has grown into a genuine backpacker and trekking hub, with a walkable town center, easy access to trailheads, and a noticeably cooler climate than the coast — a welcome break if you've just arrived from a beach stretch of your trip.

## What to do here

- **[Pekoe Trail Stage 16: Ella to Namunukula](/pekoe-trail-stage-16-ella)** — the trail's busiest, most rewarding stage, with tea-country ridgelines and a genuine climb
- **[Nine Arches Bridge](/nine-arches-bridge)** — the island's most photographed railway bridge, an easy walk from town
- **[Ravana Falls](/ravana-falls)** — a roadside waterfall with a natural pool, easily combined with a hiking day

## Where to go next

Once you've spent a couple of days here, **Haputale** is the natural next stop — cooler, quieter, and home to the next stretch of the Pekoe Trail along with two of the island's most dramatic waterfalls.
