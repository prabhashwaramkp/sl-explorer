As the trail's official starting point, Stage 1 gets a lot of first-timers — including people just testing whether hiking in Sri Lanka's heat and humidity suits them before committing to further stages. It's a fair introduction: a real climb, genuine forest, but nothing technical.

## What to expect

The first hour is the steepest, gaining most of the stage's elevation before the trail levels into rolling forest track through the Hanthana range, popular locally with university students and weekend hikers from Kandy.

## Getting there

Kandy is one of the easiest starting points on the entire trail to reach — a major rail and road hub, meaning no special arrangements needed to begin here.
