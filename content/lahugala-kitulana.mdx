Small enough to visit in an afternoon, Lahugala-Kitulana centers on ancient irrigation tanks where elephants gather to feed on aquatic grasses — a low-commitment safari that fits easily around a surf trip.

## What to expect

A short, focused visit rather than a full-day safari. Elephant sightings around the tanks are reliable, with a healthy bird population as a bonus.

## Getting there

Easily combined with time in Arugam Bay or a visit to [Kumana](/kumana) further down the coast.
