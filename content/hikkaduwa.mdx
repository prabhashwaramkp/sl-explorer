Hikkaduwa's reef sits close enough to shore that snorkeling requires no boat for the shallow sections, making it the easiest marine national park to fit into a south-coast trip — train, car, or bus straight to town, then straight into the water.

## What to expect

A mix of coral, tropical reef fish, and the occasional sea turtle in the shallows; deeper diving excursions run further out for more experienced visitors.

## Best time to go

November through April, the southwest coast's calmer, clearer season.
