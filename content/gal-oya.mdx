Gal Oya's defining feature is its boat safari across Senanayake Samudraya, Sri Lanka's largest reservoir — a genuinely different wildlife experience from the jeep-track parks, with elephants occasionally spotted swimming between the reservoir's scattered islands.

## What to expect

Most visits combine a jeep safari through the surrounding forest with a boat trip on the reservoir itself, where crocodiles and waterbirds are common alongside the elephant sightings the park is known for.

## Best time to go

March through September is generally the most favourable window for both the boat safari and land-based wildlife viewing.
