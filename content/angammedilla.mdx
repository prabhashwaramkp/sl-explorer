Angammedilla protects dry evergreen forest around the Parakrama Samudra watershed — one of the Cultural Triangle's least-known parks, better suited to adventurous wildlife travelers than a first safari.

## What to expect

Elephant, sambar, spotted and barking deer, buffalo, and wild boar are regular sightings, with occasional leopard and sloth bear. A 4WD jeep is recommended given the terrain.

## Best time to go

June through September.
