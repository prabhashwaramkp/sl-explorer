Flood Plains protects a stretch of wetland along the Mahaweli River, Sri Lanka's longest — valuable as a wildlife corridor and for its birdlife, though visitor infrastructure here is thinner than the mainstream parks nearby.

## What to expect

Elephants and a strong wetland bird population, with conditions that shift considerably depending on seasonal flooding.

## Before you go

Visitor access can be restricted at times — check current arrangements with the Department of Wildlife Conservation before planning a visit here specifically.
