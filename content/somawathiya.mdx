Somawathiya pairs wildlife with pilgrimage — the ancient Somawathiya Chaitiya, one of Sri Lanka's most sacred Buddhist sites, sits within the park's floodplain wetlands, drawing both wildlife safaris and religious visitors.

## What to expect

Elephants and floodplain birdlife are the main draw, though access is genuinely more remote than the Cultural Triangle's better-known parks. Conditions vary considerably with seasonal flooding.

## Best time to go

June through September for drier, more accessible conditions.
