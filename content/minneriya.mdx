Every year as the dry season tightens water sources elsewhere, hundreds of elephants converge on the grasslands around Minneriya Tank — a seasonal spectacle known simply as "the Gathering," and one of the most reliable large-mammal events anywhere in Asia.

## What to expect

Jeep safaris circle the tank's edge rather than venturing deep into forest, so sightings tend to be open and prolonged rather than brief glimpses through trees. Waterbirds and deer round out the wildlife alongside the elephants.

## Best time to go

Late July through September, peaking around August–September as water levels drop. Outside this window, elephant numbers here are far less dramatic — see [Kaudulla](/kaudulla) as a seasonal alternative when Minneriya's gathering hasn't yet formed.
