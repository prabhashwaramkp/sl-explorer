The highest stage in this cluster, Stage 14 climbs close enough to Horton Plains to feel a genuine shift in vegetation and temperature. Pack for cold — mornings here can be sharply colder than anywhere else on the trail so far.

## What to expect

Longer and more physically demanding than Stages 12-13, with a sustained climb in the first half before a long descent toward Boralanda. Start early; afternoon cloud cover here can roll in fast and cut visibility.

## Pairing with Horton Plains

If you have an extra day, [Horton Plains National Park](/horton-plains-worlds-end) (home to World's End) sits close enough to this stage to combine into a longer highland trip.
