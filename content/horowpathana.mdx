Horowpathana remains largely undeveloped for mainstream tourism, which is precisely its appeal for travelers seeking a genuinely quiet dry-zone safari over a well-trodden one.

## What to expect

Elephants, deer, and buffalo in dense dry-zone scrub — a 4WD jeep is necessary, and sightings require more patience than the busier southern parks.

## Best time to go

June through September generally offers better conditions.
