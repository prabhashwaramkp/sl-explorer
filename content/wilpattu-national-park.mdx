Wilpattu is Sri Lanka's largest national park by area, named for the "villus" — natural sand-rimmed lakes — scattered through its dense forest and scrub. It sees a fraction of Yala's visitor traffic, which means longer, less crowded wildlife encounters for those willing to travel a bit further north.

## What to expect

The dense vegetation means sightings can require more patience than Yala's more open terrain, but leopard density here is genuinely comparable, and the experience of having a sighting largely to yourself is a real trade-off in favor of Wilpattu.

## Why choose Wilpattu over Yala

If you've already done a safari elsewhere and want a genuinely different, quieter experience — or if Yala's dry-season closure falls during your trip — Wilpattu is the natural alternative. It's less convenient to reach from the southern coast, so factor in extra travel time.

## Best time to go

February through October tends to offer the best access, with some tracks becoming difficult during the heaviest monsoon months.
