Delft feels unlike anywhere else in Sri Lanka — a flat, dry island where feral ponies (descended from Dutch-era colonial stock) roam freely among coral-stone walls, an ancient baobab tree, and Portuguese and Dutch ruins.

## What to expect

Getting there is part of the experience: a ferry or boat from the Jaffna or Kayts area, with the island itself best explored by bicycle or tuk-tuk given its size.

## Practical notes

Allow a full day given the travel time. Calmer, drier conditions (roughly January to September) make for a more comfortable crossing.
