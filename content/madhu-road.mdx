Madhu Road combines a dry-zone wildlife habitat with genuine cultural significance — the area is closely tied to the Our Lady of Madhu shrine, one of Sri Lanka's most important pilgrimage sites.

## What to expect

Elephants and deer in dry-zone forest, with access that can be more complicated than the mainstream parks — check current arrangements with the Department of Wildlife Conservation before visiting.

## Best time to go

January through September, avoiding the wettest stretches.
