At around 220 metres, Diyaluma is one of the tallest waterfalls on the island — but the real draw for most visitors is the series of natural pools at the top, reachable via a short, occasionally steep scramble up the rock face beside the falls.

## Safety note

The climb to the top pools isn't officially maintained and has resulted in serious accidents in the past, particularly in wet or crowded conditions. Go with a local guide if you're unfamiliar with the route, avoid it entirely after rain, and never get close to the actual edge of the drop.

## Practical notes

The base viewpoint (visible from the main road) requires no climbing and is worth a stop on its own if you'd rather admire the height from a safe distance.
