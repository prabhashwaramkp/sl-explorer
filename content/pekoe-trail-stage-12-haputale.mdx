Stage 12 trades the crowds of Ella for something quieter — a long ridge walk with, on a clear day, views stretching all the way to the southern coast. Haputale sits higher and cooler than Ella, and it shows in the trail: mist rolls through often, even at midday.

## What to expect

The path follows old estate roads for much of the route, climbing steadily rather than sharply. There's little shade, so the exposed sections can be tough in full sun — but those same open stretches are what give this stage its views.

## Trail status

Check current conditions before setting out — this stretch has occasionally been affected by seasonal mist reducing visibility on the ridge sections, though the path itself is generally well maintained.

## Where to base yourself

Haputale town, at the end of this stage, is a good overnight base for tackling Stages 13 and 14 the following days.
