Kumana sits adjacent to Yala's wilderness but draws a different crowd — serious birders, drawn by lagoon nesting colonies that rank among the best birdwatching in South Asia, alongside a respectable chance of elephant and leopard sightings.

## What to expect

May through July is peak nesting season, when the lagoons fill with herons, pelicans, painted storks, and dozens of other species in dense colonies. Outside this window, it functions as a quieter, leopard-and-elephant safari park.

## Getting there

Most visitors access Kumana from Arugam Bay or Pottuvil, making it an easy add-on to an east-coast surf trip, or via Yala/Tissamaharama from the south.
