No jeep required here — Galway's Land is a small pocket of montane forest within easy walking distance of central Nuwara Eliya, popular for a short birding walk rather than a full safari day.

## What to expect

Endemic highland bird species are the main draw, in a cool, quiet forest setting that contrasts nicely with the open grassland of nearby [Horton Plains](/horton-plains-worlds-end).

## Best time to go

January through April tends to be the most pleasant, drier stretch.
