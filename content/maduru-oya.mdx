Maduru Oya combines wildlife safari with a genuine sense of history — its reservoirs are ancient irrigation works, and the surrounding dry-zone forest holds archaeological remains alongside its elephant, deer, and buffalo populations.

## What to expect

A 4WD jeep is the practical way to explore; the terrain and remoteness make this a better fit for travelers who've already done the more accessible parks and want something quieter.

## Best time to go

June through September is generally preferable.
