While Bentota's coastal retreats lean into traditional Ayurveda, hill-country wellness stays trade on something different: cool mountain air, tea-terrace views, and a slower pace that suits yoga, meditation, and lighter wellness programs rather than intensive detox therapy.

## What's different here

Expect more emphasis on yoga, guided walks through tea estates, and meditation, with Ayurveda treatments offered as a complement rather than the core program. The cooler climate (Nuwara Eliya sits at over 1,800 metres) also makes physical activity more comfortable than the humid coast.

## A natural pairing

This region overlaps directly with the upper stages of the Pekoe Trail — a genuinely popular combination is a few days of trekking followed by a short wellness stay to recover before heading back down to the coast.

## What to ask before booking

Confirm whether a program is genuinely structured (set daily schedule, qualified instructors) or more of a general spa-hotel offering marketed as "wellness" — the two vary a lot in this region, and pricing doesn't always reflect the difference.
