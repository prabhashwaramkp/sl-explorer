A short boat ride from Nilaveli, Pigeon Island protects one of Sri Lanka's healthiest coral reefs — and is one of the few places on the island where spotting a blacktip reef shark while snorkeling is a genuine, regular occurrence rather than a rare bonus.

## What to expect

Shallow, clear water makes this accessible to snorkelers of any level; gear is rented easily at Nilaveli before the boat crossing. The reef itself is compact, so a half-day covers it comfortably.

## Best time to go

May through September, when the east coast generally has its calmest, clearest conditions.
