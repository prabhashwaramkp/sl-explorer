Local legend ties Ussangoda's strange red-earth landscape to Ravana's mythical flying chariot landing here — geologists offer a more prosaic explanation, but either way, the grassland-topped sea cliff is unlike anywhere else on the south coast.

## What to expect

A short, easy walk rather than a safari — the appeal here is purely the unusual landscape and coastal views, not wildlife density.

## A natural pairing

Close enough to [Bundala](/bundala) to combine into a single day exploring the Hambantota coastline.
