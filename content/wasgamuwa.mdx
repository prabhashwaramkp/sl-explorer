Wasgamuwa sees a fraction of the visitor traffic of Minneriya or Kaudulla, trading some sighting reliability for a genuinely wilder, more remote safari experience with healthy elephant herds and far fewer other jeeps.

## What to expect

Denser vegetation than the open-tank parks means more patience required, but deer, buffalo, and strong birdlife are consistent sightings alongside elephants.

## Best time to go

June through September tends to offer the best access and visibility.
